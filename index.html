Payments & refunds

How payment works

Clear dates, no surprises. Every family pays exactly the listed price.

Three steps

  1. RegisterFill out the registration form and choose early-bird or pay in full.
  2. Pay the $50 depositYou'll get an invoice by email. Your child's spot is held once the deposit is paid.
  3. Pay the balanceBy invoice, due January 18, 2027. Early-bird families pay in full by December 15, 2026. All payments are made online; no cash at class.

Refund policy

  • Before January 18, 2027: full refund, minus the $50 deposit.
  • Deposit: non-refundable, unless we cancel the session or your child's group is full.
  • After January 18, 2027: no refunds. For an injury or medical reason with a doctor's note, unused classes become credit toward the next session.
  • Missed classes: can be made up during the session. They don't carry over to the next one.
Safety first

Your child is in good hands

Insured

Liability insurance is in place before the first class.

Background checked

Everyone who works with students passes a background check before class begins and follows a written child-safety policy.

Safe sparring

Light contact only, with headgear, gloves, mouthguard and foot pads. No sweeps or takedowns.

Questions

Good to know

Does my child need experience?

No. Classes are built for beginners, and every student starts at the same level.

How do I pay?

After you register, you'll get an invoice by email with a secure pay button for the $50 deposit. The balance is invoiced and due by January 18, 2027. All payments are made online.

What if we miss a class?

Missed classes can be made up during the session.

What should my child wear?

Comfortable workout clothes are fine to start. Uniforms will be offered later as an optional add-on.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to watch class.
